How it works, in plain words.

Read this before our call. It answers what most agencies ask.

What results should I expect?

I don't promise a number of meetings. The first weeks go into testing which message works, and you see every number every week. On BDR the $250 meeting fee is only for qualified meetings that are actually held. If your typical project is $15,000 or more, one signed client can cover several months of fees.

What exactly do I pay?

BD Partner: $2,500 a month plus 5% of what you actually collect on the first contract with each company we bring you, 2% above the first $100,000 collected from one client, with no per-meeting fee. No deal, no percentage. BDR: $1,000 a month plus $250 for every qualified meeting that is held, no commission. No-shows are free. Signals: $400 a month, month to month, cancel anytime. All invoiced in USD, paid by bank transfer.

Is there a commission on BDR?

No. BDR is $1,000 a month plus $250 per held qualified meeting. Every deal you close from our meetings is 100% yours.

What counts as a qualified meeting?

A decision-maker (founder, CEO, head of sales or whoever signs) at a company that fits the profile we agree on, who shows up. We write the definition down together before we start. No-shows do not count and are not billed, and we try to rebook them. After each meeting we ask you one quick question, was it qualified, so we both have a clean record. You confirm within 24 hours; if we don't hear back within 3 business days, it counts as qualified.

What does “5% of what you collect” mean on BD Partner?

You pay our share only when your customer pays you. Paid upfront: we get 5% once. Paid monthly: 5% of each payment. Example: you sign a customer at $3,000 a month, you collect $3,000, we get $150 for that month.

What if the deal is very large?

Only BD Partner has a percentage, and it drops on big deals: 5% on the first $100,000 you collect from a client and 2% on anything above that. Example: a $300,000 client means $5,000 + $4,000 = $9,000, not $15,000. Signals and BDR have no percentage at all.

How long does the percentage apply?

On BD Partner, on what you collect in the first 12 months of that first contract. After that, and on any later contracts with the same company, it is 100% yours.

Which deals count for the percentage?

On BD Partner, the first contract with each new company that met you through us, or that we contacted for you, signed within 6 months of our first contact. Your existing clients and companies you are already talking to are never included: you send us that list before we start and we leave them out.

Which plan should I start with?

We are a new BD partner, so every plan is month to month (the one exception: BD Partner for agencies with $100k+ projects): you can judge the work on your own market before you commit to anything longer. If your team already does its own outreach, start with Signals. If you want meetings on your calendar, BDR. If you want us to run the deal with you to a signed contract, BD Partner.

What if a company we contacted reaches out to you directly later?

On BD Partner, it counts for the percentage, as long as you sign within 6 months of our first contact. Every first contact is logged with its date in our shared tracker, so it is easy to check together.

When and how do I pay the percentage?

On BD Partner, within 7 days of collecting from your customer. You log each payment (date and amount) in the shared tracker within 7 days, and we invoice you. We may ask for the matching invoice to your customer.

What if my customer gets a refund?

If your customer gets their money back within the first 30 days, we refund our share of that payment.

When do you start?

Live within 7 days, with nothing to set up on your side. On BDR and BD Partner the month starts counting from our first outreach, not the day you pay. Week 1 is setup: your ideal client profile, lists and messages, all approved by you.

What do you need from me?

Approve messages and lists within 48 hours, show up to the meetings we book, and update the outcome in the shared tracker.

Who owns the lists and the setup?

You do. On BDR and BD Partner, the whole outreach setup is included in the plan and is transferred to you if we stop working together. We never touch your main domain.

What if I cancel?

Signals is month to month and you can cancel anytime. BDR and BD Partner are month to month with 30 days’ notice. The one exception: on BD Partner, agencies with $100k+ projects work with a 6-month commitment. On BD Partner, meetings and contacts from before you cancel still follow the same 6-month and 12-month rules.

How do I pay?

USD invoice, paid by bank transfer.

Do you guarantee meetings?

No. No honest BD partner guarantees meeting counts. We commit to the work, the speed and full transparency. You judge the work on meetings booked, and you see every number weekly.
